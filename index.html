Echo rotation reading: expert pilot

34 trials. Plan on 30 to 40 minutes. You can stop and come back.

What you will see

  • Each trial shows two apical recordings from the same patient, A and B.
  • Imagine you recorded A, then rotated the probe about its long axis and recorded B.
  • Say which way the probe rotated from A to B: clockwise or counterclockwise.
  • Use the convention you use at the bedside.
  • Some pairs differ a lot, some a little, and some not at all.
  • If you cannot see a rotation, choose Can't tell. That is a useful answer.
  • Then rate your confidence: low, medium or high.
  • Say whether both recordings are four-chamber views.
  • Say which recording is more suitable for assessing LV function, and why.

Please

  • Use a laptop or desktop screen, so A and B sit side by side.
  • Judge from the images only. There is no feedback and no time limit.
  • Do not go back to earlier trials. Your first reading is what we need.
  • Keep this folder together. Do not rename or move the clip files.
  • Your answers are sent to the study team at the break and at the end. If sending fails, the page asks you to download the file and email it.
